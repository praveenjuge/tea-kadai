Tea Kadai

A cozy pixel-art shop sim about running a little tea kadai on a busy Chennai street.

For your computer Download for Mac Intel and Apple Silicon For your computer Download for Windows Windows 10 and 11 For your computer Download for Linux 64-bit

Tea Kadai is a computer game. Open this page on a Mac, Windows or Linux PC to download it.

Make tea by hand

Hold to pour and let go in the green. Plain, ginger, lemon, and filter coffee once you grow.

Fry vadai and bajji

Drop a batch in the kadai and lift them out when they turn golden. Not before, not after.

Meet your regulars

Karthik, a broke engineering student, drops by every day. Remember his usual and he brings friends.

Grow the kadai

Hire helpers, buy a bench and string lights, catch golden glasses, and open branches across Chennai.

Opening it the first time

It only takes a minute.

Mac

Open the download, then double-click Tea Kadai. That's it.

Windows

Double-click the download. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. You'll only see this once.

Linux

Unpack the download and double-click Tea Kadai inside.
